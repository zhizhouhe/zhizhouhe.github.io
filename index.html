About

I am a Senior Research Fellow at the 5G/6G Innovation Centre (5GIC/6GIC), University of Surrey, where I also received my Ph.D. in Information and Wireless Communication Systems. My research builds AI-native radio access networks: agentic, intent-driven intelligence for the O-RAN RIC, digital twins for safe training of xApp controllers, and reinforcement-learning and generative methods for radio-resource management.

I lead the technical work on projects under the EPSRC CHEDDAR 6G Hub (ORAN-TWIN, ORAN-TWIN-X and Assurance O-RAN) and on industry-funded work on LLM pipelines, and I work closely with partners including Keysight, Samsung and Huawei. I also mentor Ph.D. students in our group.
